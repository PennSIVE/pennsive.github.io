# Onboarding

## Welcome to PennSIVE!
<br>

New employees should be aware of the following as you are beginning your journey: 

  - Workday@Penn
  - Penn card registration
  - 0365 Email set up
  - Request Building access 
  - Computer for use
  - Listserv Communications 
    - myHR, Wednesdays, bi-weekly, 3 Things
    - Newsletters
    - Brown Bag Sessions
    - Penn Competency Library
  - HR provides access to software needed for your projects 
  - Connect with your Faculty for Workday Trainings 
    - CITI Certifications Training 
    - HIPPA
  - Benefit Enrollments
    - Benefits/Insurance Plans: Healthcare, Financial Security, Wellbeing, Retirement 
    - Penn Cobalt 
    - Penn Employee Assistance Program
    - Public Service Loan Forgiveness 

<br>

**HR** will provide you with instructions for Penn Card registration + pickup, request security access, and provide a computer for use. 

**PMACS**, our academic computer specialists with Penn Medicine, will be responsible for aligning your device with the Penn network and more.

### Printing in the Lab 

10.156.159.189 is the IP Address for the printer. To set up access, you must first connect to the VPN, then add the printer via the printing settings on your device. <br>
To find your scanned items from the Printer please see your U:Drive folder. It can be accessed via the small icon. 


### ISC BOX Storage Upgrades needed?

Up to 5TB are provided. Email ISC to request storage at help (at) isc.upenn.edu

### ADOBE Licenses 

For Faculty and Staff, Adobe licenses are active and included. More [here](https://isc.upenn.edu/resources/adobe-creative-cloud) on ISC’s webpage. 

For Students, postdocs, and others the following offer can be requested for purchase: [PowerPDF](https://www.tungstenautomation.com/products/power-pdf#cq=i1)

Adobe access can also be requested for Adobe Acrobat and Illustrator needs to make posters.
